## Soundness (Assumption 1, first 300 scenes)

Logging is passive (300/300 outcomes identical). 0.10 % of executed
certified rows leave the tube, i.e. the ε_k quantile holds at the rate it
was calibrated for (p99 x 1.25). No violation happens during a fallback
(ShortStop, MPC, CBF: 0 of 11 / 10 / 7), so Assumption 3 holds after the
hold anchor fix. Both ShortStop violations in this set are certified rows
whose real motion left the tube (deviation 15-51 mm vs ε_k 10-19 mm) while
the arm pushed or lifted a block: contact, which the kinematic f-hat does
not model. Theorem 1 therefore holds with high probability, as stated in
`theory_audit.md` item 2; the residual 1 % is the tube's tail under contact.
