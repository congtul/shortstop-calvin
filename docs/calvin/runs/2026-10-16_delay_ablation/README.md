# Delay stress test, component ablation, horizon sweep

Registered before the runs: [`../../lab_notebook.md`](../../lab_notebook.md), sections
"Delay stress test" and "Component ablation and horizon sensitivity".
Final parameters, nothing tuned, one rollout per scene, same policy seeds as
the final. Delay: first 200 `final` scenes, 2026-10-14 14:24 -> 2026-10-15
13:04 (interrupted 02:24-05:44 by a Windows Update restart; resumed, every
scene is seeded on its own). Ablation and H sweep: first 300 `final`
scenes, 2026-10-15 14:35 -> 2026-10-17 (paused once, resumed). L0, L4 and
H = 4 are the final rows of those scenes; the H = 4 smoke rerun matched them
exactly. Generated by `python scripts/analyze_calvin_extra.py` (LaTeX rows,
also written to `docs/paper/shortstop/tables/`); outputs in
`outputs/calvin_final/`.

Findings:
- Delay: without H = r + d, violations grow with d (ShortStop 2.0 / 14.0 /
  31.5 % at d = 1 / 2 / 3; MPC and CBF alike); certifying H = 4 + d keeps
  them <= 0.5 % (CBF <= 1.5 %) at a cost of 2-6 points of safe success for
  d <= 2. ShortStop vs MPC / CBF (aware) within +-4.5 points; one of six
  comparisons p < 0.05 (CBF +4.5 at d = 1).
- Ablation: certification alone (L1) removes collisions but falls back in
  95 % of decisions (27.7 % safe success, 217/300 timeouts). K = 4 and g(a)
  add +0.0 and +1.0 (MDT samples nearly identical). Repair adds +50.7
  (152 scenes won, 0 lost); its 2 collisions are the contact cases of the
  soundness audit.
- Horizon: H = 6 / 8 lose 6.0 / 8.7 points (18 vs 0, 29 vs 3 discordant),
  violations unchanged (2 / 1 / 2 scenes): H = r without delay.
